# Index & Roadmap

## The question this guide answers

How did physicists get from *firing particles through slits* to
*manufacturing a quantum computer*? And why does the second one
work at all?

## The four rules

Everything below is in service of establishing these. They are the
whole payload — the rest is how we found out.

1. **Two outcomes, never a middle.** Ask the question, get one of
   two answers.
2. **Before you ask, it carries a number for each option, and those
   numbers can cancel.** Not probabilities — probabilities only add.
3. **Find out which option, and the cancelling stops.** Permanently.
4. **You can adjust it and read it on demand.** (Rules 1–3 are
   nature. Rule 4 is engineering, and took until 1938.)
